# The sample heading that isn't bold, on the live app, 2026-10-20

The sample `northvale-whisky-warning-not-bold` prints "GOVERNMENT WARNING:" in capitals but
not in bold. It was checked 5 times in a row with its sample button, on revision
`ttb-label-verifier--0000029` (image `6bbcffc`, 2 CPUs, 4 GiB, where "clearly heavier" meant
1.1 times), from a home connection, with a short script that posts the button and reads the
warning's row on the results page.

| Run | Time | Claude said the heading | Measured | Warning |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 3.4 s | can't tell | | Check this |
| 2 | 2.7 s | bold | no heavier than the text | Check this |
| 3 | 3.2 s | can't tell | | Check this |
| 4 | 2.8 s | bold | no heavier than the text | Check this |
| 5 | 2.7 s | bold | no heavier than the text | Check this |

Check this all 5 times. Claude called the heading bold in 3 of the 5 runs; each time the
measured stroke thickness sent it to review ("looks bold to the AI, but measured, its letters
are no thicker than the rest of the warning"). Without the measurement those 3 would have
been false passes. Tesseract's words were ready in time in all 5 runs.
